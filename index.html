--About me

Hello, Guys!. My name is Muhammad Nurul Huda, you can call me Huda. Nowdays, i'm a maths teacher in a private college called Star Winner in Gamping, Sleman. The students are senior high level and they're coming from diverse schools nearby. I love maths, but not as smart as maths prodigy like Terence Tao or at least olympiad students. I'm just a simple human. I have other skill, such as programming and chess.

This website is built for some reasons. First: There is site for recording any maths recognitions. Sometimes maybe I need those anymore. So, I think it's important. Second: I don't know how long I live in this life. I want to enjoy life with wisdom on every second. Writing moments is a powerful activity to appreciate life and raise emotional side of myself. When I'm in the old age, I can nostalgic to every moments that have been written in diaries. I like memorizing back scenes of my life just to recall or get a good thing to the future scenes.

I need these records. I don't want to live with only through it, but it has to be learned because there was many patterns in life. There were lots of bad things that I've done and I've received the consequences. I need a revolution of my self. Therefore, to the readers (whoever you're), either my wife, my children, friends, somebody, or even my self, I want you to remember this: "You become the withness that I won't give up to be good person."
